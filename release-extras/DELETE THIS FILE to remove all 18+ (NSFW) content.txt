Reframe — 18+ content switch

While this file is here, Reframe works normally: the 18+ (adult) site list stays
hidden until someone turns it on in Settings and confirms they are 18 or older.

DELETE THIS FILE to remove every 18+ part of Reframe:
  - the built-in adult sites disappear from the Downloader
  - the "Show 18+ sites" switch disappears from Settings and can never be turned on

Links in your own .txt files (Documents > Reframe Other Sites) still show,
because those are yours.

To bring 18+ back, put a file with exactly this name back next to Reframe.exe
and restart Reframe.
